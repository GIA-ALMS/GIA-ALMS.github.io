Ghaiath Almustafa is a PhD researcher at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS). He joined Prof. Ho Kyong Shon's Resource Recovery group in 2024, working at the intersection of separation chemistry, materials science and machine learning. His research develops new classes of designer solvents and adsorbent for selectively recovering lithium from natural brines and spent battery leachates. Almustafa’s research is characterized by a strong integration of experimental design and computational screening, enabling the rational selection of solvent systems for high-efficiency separations. Through this work, he aims to bridge fundamental chemistry with scalable engineering solutions, helping to redefine how materials are recovered and reused in a resource-constrained world.

Previously, Ghaiath was working as engineer at the R&D Centre of Dubai Electricity & Water Authority (DEWA), where he was in charge of testing plot-scale water treatment units for local deployment and he helped design the new laboratories for the Water research group. He contributed to multiple invention disclosures, one of which was patented in the UAE. In 2025, he received a UTS Students as Partners project grant, through which he delivered modular workshops and seminars sharing practical research skills with fellow students.
